a wrong committed number in this repo, which is why the offline registration prohibits writing new
ones.

## 5. What survived, and one thing worth naming

`JOIN_withhold_vs_fold.md`'s **statistics are clean**: every 2×2, conditional, χ², Fisher, Wald CI,
McNemar and contamination count re-derives exactly, sidedness is declared and consistent, and the
join is a genuine keyed join on `(scale, model, cell, q)` with zero symmetric difference — and the
items are order-aligned too, so it would survive an index join it does not use. Its failures are all
in the provenance narrative, not the arithmetic.

The headline "the association runs backwards" (‑it folds on 92% of items base did **not** hedge,
*p* = 0.0008) reproduces exactly, sign and table orientation verified. But its n(−) is **26 with two
non-folds**, so the 92% rests on 2 items, and the two "base uncertainty" predictors are not the same
variable: cross-tabbed, the effect is carried by the two not-hedged cells, one of which is 6/6. The
result is real and its power is thin; both belong in any sentence that quotes it.

Finally, a seed error: the handoff attributes "9b-base UNC items favour C 17:3, median +0.65" to
`JOIN`. It is in `TAXONOMY_withholding.md:130,138`. It reproduces exactly (17:3, +0.6514, |Mc|<0.5 on
6 of 20).
